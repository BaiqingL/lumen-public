Quiet Light — Lumen demonstration files

Original twelve-second light animation and synthesized audio created for Lumen playback, display-mode and subtitle checks. No external footage, commercial film, personal media or fake app screens are included.

  • Lumen - Quiet Light.mp4 (2,928,245 bytes)
  • Lumen - Quiet Light (Letterbox).mp4 (2,261,095 bytes)
  • Lumen - Quiet Light.en.srt (261 bytes)
  • Lumen - Quiet Light.zh-CN.srt (276 bytes)

Both MP4 files are 1920×1080, 24 fps, H.264 video with AAC stereo sound. The Letterbox file has encoded black bars above and below a 1920×804 picture.

Open in Lumen

Download a video through your browser, then select it through Lumen’s normal system file picker. Download an English or Chinese SRT and import it using Lumen’s subtitle control. On the letterbox video, compare Intelligent Fill and Letterbox after a few seconds of playback. Subtitles are separate files, not embedded in the picture.
